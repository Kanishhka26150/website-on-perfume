04 • Raw Ingredients

Natural Palette & Materials

Pure, ethically harvested ingredients sourced directly from sustainable floral farms.

French Rose de Mai

Harvested in Grasse at dawn in May. Requires 5,000kg of fresh rose petals to yield 1kg of essential oil.

Atlas Cedar & Oakmoss

Sourced from high-altitude forests, bringing grounded woody notes and rich, moist green earthy depth.
